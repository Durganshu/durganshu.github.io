Hey there, I'm Durganshu Mishra, a Computational Sciences and Engineering student at the Technical University of Munich. My academic journey? It basically started with mechanical engineering and has now evolved into a passionate pursuit of High-Performance Computing.

But that's not all. I'm also a "Hilfswissenschaftler" (German for Research Assistant) at the Chair of Computer Architecture and Parallel Systems at the Technical University of Munich. Here, I'm contributing towards a range of [Deep-Sea projects](https://deep-projects.eu/). Want to know more? Please take a deep dive into our research escapades on the [Chair's webpage](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/caps/homepage/); it's where the magic happens.

Now, for my secret life: when I'm not decoding the mysteries of the computational universe, I'm a die-hard fan of FC Barcelona's football prowess and the Indian National Cricket Team's legendary cricketing battles. And here's a little secret: I sometimes swap my keyboard for sports shoes and join the action myself.

So, welcome to my digital playground! This is where you can keep up with my projects, research adventures, and the latest tales I've spun in my newfound passion for blogging. Stick around! You might discover some computational sorcery!
