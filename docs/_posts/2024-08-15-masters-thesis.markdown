I’m excited to share that I’ve officially completed my Master’s degree in Computational Science and Engineering from the Technical University of Munich, marked by the submission of my Master's thesis. 🎓

My thesis focused on enhancing the <strong>[sys-sage](https://github.com/caps-tum/sys-sage)</strong> library to support quantum technologies by serving as a unified interface for quantum system representation. It involved integrating the [Quantum Device Management Interface (QDMI)](https://www.cda.cit.tum.de/research/quantum/qdmi/) into the [Munich Quantum Software Stack (MQSS)](https://www.munich-quantum-valley.de/research/research-areas/mqss), with a targeted implementation for IBM backends. The work introduced static and dynamic topologies, extended the sys-sage API, and proposed a high-level framework for managing quantum devices more effectively. You can read the full thesis [here](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/1763170).

As a new chapter begins, I’ve joined SevenCs in Hamburg as a C++ Software Developer, where I’ll be contributing to the Nautilus SDK and Pathfinder ECDIS. Looking forward to what lies ahead!
