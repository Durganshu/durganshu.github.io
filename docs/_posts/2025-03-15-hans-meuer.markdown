Thrilled to share that our research paper, based on my Master's thesis work, has been accepted for presentation at [ISC High Performance 2025](https://isc-hpc.com/program/) in Hamburg — one of the most prestigious conferences in the HPC community. Even more exciting: our paper was selected for the [Hans Meuer Award](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/caps/aktuelles/news-single-view/article/isc-hans-meuer-award-goes-to-our-paper-towards-a-unified-architectural-representation-in-hpcqc-extending-sys-sage-for-quantum-technologies/), which recognizes outstanding research contributions by young scientists in the field of high performance computing. 🏆

I'll be presenting the paper on **June 10, 2025**, as part of ISC's main program. The work extends the [sys-sage](https://github.com/caps-tum/sys-sage) library to provide a unified architectural abstraction for HPC and quantum computing systems. This builds upon the foundational work from my Master’s thesis and reflects the collaborative efforts within the [Munich Quantum Software Stack (MQSS)](https://www.munich-quantum-valley.de/research/research-areas/mqss) and the [QDMI initiative](https://www.cda.cit.tum.de/research/quantum/qdmi/).

Check out how [LRZ is connecting the dots at ISC 2025](https://www.lrz.de/en/latest-news/events/the-lrz-at-isc-2025-connectingthedots), and here’s a shoutout from the [ISC LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/isc-hpc_isc25-connectingthedots-hansmeueraward-activity-7308819764909342721-Bbq2/) announcing the award.

Looking forward to presenting and engaging with the HPC and quantum computing communities at ISC!
